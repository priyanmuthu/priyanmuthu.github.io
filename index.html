About

        

          I am a Research Scientist in the
          AI/ML
          group at Apple, working on intelligent user interfaces. I received my
          Ph.D. from Harvard, where I was advised by
          Prof. Elena Glassman
          at the
          Variation Lab.
          My research lies in the intersection of Human-Computer Interaction, Artificial Intelligence, and
          Software Engineering.
        

        

          Prior to this, I was a Master's Student at UCSD,
          where I worked with
          Prof. Philip Guo
          at the
          UCSD Design Lab.
          I was also fortunate to work in
          Microsoft Research
          India as a Research Fellow for two years, where I worked on projects related to
          information retrieval, developer tools, and programming accessibility.
